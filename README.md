## About me

I've spent over 17 years in higher education and research. I love to teach, and I love to learn!

I'm a scientist with a background spanning materials science, physical chemistry, and engineering. I'm interested in understanding how materials behave, how processes work, and how computational methods can help us investigate both.

My published research spans experimental materials science, mathematical modelling, and the exploration of generative AI in higher education. I'm currently expanding my computational interests into process simulation and AI applications in materials research.
